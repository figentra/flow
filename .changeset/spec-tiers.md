`vellum gate` carries the spec tiers of Stackra ADR 0502: `**Tier**` (trivial, small, feature, big), the lane check's floor from the diff (migrations, contracts, permissions, money and new members raise it), the approval stops (`Approved by <who> <date>`, feature before Tasked, big before Planned and Tasked, with `**Approver**`), and small specs that keep their plan and tasks in spec.md. The spec template and the clarify and plan commands carry the same fields and the approval stop.
